How it works

FIND

Find a partner

Browse players for your sport and start a chat. Perfect for tennis, badminton, squash and table tennis.

ORGANISE

Create a match

Set the place, time and number of players. Split teams fairly with one tap and pick team colours.

PLAY

Chat and play

Every match and session has its own group chat, so everyone stays in the loop. Then just turn up and play.

Built for real, casual sport

You set the time. No fixed weekly slots — play when it suits you.

Real Cambridge venues. Local courts and pitches, with booking links where available.

Group chat built in. Organise everything in one place, not across five apps.

No payments in the app. Where a game has a cost, you pay the organiser directly.
