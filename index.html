How it works

FIND

Find a partner

Browse players for your sport, see their level and when they are free, then start a chat. Perfect for tennis, badminton, squash and table tennis.

ORGANISE

Create a match

Set the place, time and number of players. Split teams fairly with one tap and pick team colours.

PLAY

Chat and play

Every match and session has its own group chat, so everyone stays in the loop. Then just turn up and play.

Built for real, casual sport

You set the time. No fixed weekly slots. Say when you are free and let others find you.

Real Cambridge venues. Local courts, pitches and pools on the map, with directions and booking links where available.

Messages and notifications. Know straight away when someone messages you or invites you to a game.

Group chat built in. Organise everything in one place, not across five apps.

Match history and ratings. Rate the game afterwards and keep a record of what you have played.

No payments in the app. Where a game has a cost, you pay the organiser directly. No adverts either.
